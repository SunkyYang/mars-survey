Hi, and thanks for giving this a try. We put it together so you can build a conjoint survey like our premium SUV one without starting from scratch.

You only need three things: why you're collecting the data, the attributes and their levels, and the profiles you'd like people to rate. The rest is already in place. If you'd rather not begin with a blank page, Start from the example fills in our SUV survey for you to adapt.

Anything on a grey background can be changed. Click ✎, make your edit, then press Done or Enter. The preview on the right shows exactly what your respondents will see.

The standard wording between your questions adjusts to your product and numbers on its own (marked default). Feel free to rewrite any of it; your version then stays as you wrote it (marked custom). **bold** and __underline__ work too.

Your work is saved in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back later. If anything looks off or you get stuck, message Sunky. Have fun with it.
