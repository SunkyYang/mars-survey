How it works

  1. Create an account with your invite code.
  2. In the editor, fill in why you are collecting the data, the attributes and their levels, and the profiles (combinations) respondents will rate. A live preview shows the survey as you type.
  3. Publish and share the survey link.
  4. Log in any time to see responses and download CSV files.

Every survey has the same structure as our premium SUV conjoint survey: welcome, name and NetID, up to 3 background questions, attribute description, attribute importance (1 to 7) and profile ratings (1 to 7). Every question is required.
